01 Critical Headlines

Patch Tuesday, record edition — 974 vulnerabilities, two under active exploitation

Critical

Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday is the largest in company history, with 113 rated Critical. Two elevation-of-privilege flaws are already being weaponised in the wild, both now sitting in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.

CVE-2026-85880 Windows ALPC EoP — escapes AppContainer sandbox to SYSTEM
CVE-2026-81963 Windows Update Stack link-following EoP to SYSTEM

Android took 180 patches this cycle, including a no-interaction RCE in the System component.

Federal remediation deadline: Sept 22, 2026. Patch Windows endpoints this week, no exceptions.

AI is compressing the ransomware kill chain to under 30 minutes

Critical

Check Point research and our own heatmap data agree: some intrusions now go from initial access to full domain compromise in as little as 30 minutes, with 10 hours as the new upper bound. Two groups drove this week's volume — "The Gentlemen" against Air Canada and pharma targets, and "Fiasco Group" running double-extortion across sectors.

Ransom payment rate sits at a multi-year low of 23%, but overall attack volume is still up 24.9% year-over-year. Fewer payouts is not the same as fewer incidents.

GTA VI "leaks" are a malware delivery vector, not a gaming story

User Risk

A large campaign is distributing ransomware, infostealers, and RATs disguised as early-access or leaked builds of Grand Theft Auto VI. Wherever personal downloads on corporate devices go unmanaged, this is a live insider-risk vector worth a five-minute reminder to staff.

04 What High-Performing SOCs Are Doing Differently

The dominant 2026 attacker profile isn't smash-and-grab, it's burrow-and-persist — Process Injection has held the #1 technique spot for a third straight year. Four shifts separate the teams keeping pace:

01  ·  Coverage to Relevance

Deep detection over broad coverage

100% ATT&CK matrix coverage is a vanity metric. What matters is deep, validated detection on the 15–20 techniques your actual adversaries use.

02  ·  Identity-First Detection

Monitor before attackers touch your systems

Infostealer logs, stolen session cookies, and dark-web credential monitoring are becoming primary detection vectors — before attackers ever touch the environment.

03  ·  Continuous Validation

Weekly automated checks, not quarterly audits

Purple teaming and breach-and-attack simulation are moving from quarterly exercises to weekly automated checks.

04  ·  Finished Intelligence

TTPs over raw IOC feeds

Raw IOC feeds generate noise. TTP-mapped, finished intelligence drives focused, actionable investigations that cut through alert fatigue.
