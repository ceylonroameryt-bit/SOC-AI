## 1. Guiding Principles & Platform Legal Posture
SOC-AI is designed as an ethical, legally responsible cybersecurity aggregation index. We operate on the foundational principle that **public accessibility or existence of an RSS feed does not automatically equate to unrestricted commercial reuse.**

Attribution is a mandatory baseline requirement, but **attribution is not a substitute for authorization.** We systematically record and enforce permission evidence for every qualified source indexed on our platform.

## 2. Ingestion & Content Display Standards
For every source indexed in our canonical registry, our platform enforces technical and legal constraints matching its documented license:

1. **Title & Snippet Indexing Only:**
   - We do NOT scrape, cache, or republish full-text articles unless covered by explicit public domain or open licenses.
   - For all commercial publishers and syndicated feeds, summaries are strictly capped (between 280 and 500 characters) to convey factual event context without replacing the publisher's work.
2. **Prominent Publisher Attribution:**
   - Every report prominently displays the publisher's official name, verified publisher domain, and original publication timestamp.
3. **Mandatory Canonical Deep-Linking:**
   - Every report includes a direct hyperlink forwarding the user to the publisher's original article URL. We do not use intermediary paywalls, link cloaking, or frame embedding that deprives publishers of direct traffic.
4. **Image & Asset Safeguards:**
   - We do not hotlink or republish copyrighted editorial images from external publishers without explicit permission or applicable open licenses.

## 3. Permission Categories in Our Registry
Our canonical source registry tracks four distinct permission classifications:
- **`permitted_intended_use` (149 Sources):** Verified public domain government feeds (e.g., CISA, UK NCSC OGL v3.0, NIST), explicit open-access security research, or RSS syndication feeds operating under terms authorizing headlines and snippet discovery with back-links.
- **`restricted` (21 Sources):** Feeds with heightened terms requiring strict character caps, non-commercial restrictions, or prohibition of downstream redistribution.
- **`denied` (28 Sources):** Endpoints with terms explicitly barring automated indexing, commercial aggregation, or AI processing. These are quarantined or rejected and excluded from ingestion.
- **`pending` (857 Backlog Candidates):** Candidate feeds awaiting formal technical and legal assessment. **Pending feeds are strictly excluded from active scheduled ingestion and target metrics.**
